Preview Privacy, Terms & Sources

MR-S10 Railway Systems is an independent technology and R&D project. This protected prototype does not claim official affiliation with MRL, ECRL, any rail operator or government agency.

Location and privacy

  • My Location requests one foreground device position only when you tap it and allow browser permission. There is no background watch.
  • Clear / Hide Location invalidates pending replies and removes the stored in-page fix, blue marker and accuracy ring. It does not move your chosen map camera. Use Reset if you also want to return to the network overview.
  • The location feature does not write coordinates to persistent storage, GitHub, Supabase, analytics or another user's account. It is separate from private Owner calibration.
  • The existing basemap and CDN providers receive normal network metadata and viewport tile requests. Centering on your location can reveal an approximate viewed area through tile requests. Provider logs/retention are controlled by those providers and are not represented as known here.
  • Existing Vercel protection and separate Gateway authentication may use their own cookies/session storage. This Preview does not change those systems. No new third-party tracking is embedded.

Terms and decision-support limits

Use this Preview for review and preliminary research only. Source-reference station points, schematic records, service adjacency, physical track geometry and operational status are distinct. Unknown fields are not approvals or evidence that freight operations have begun. Do not use the map for train operations, navigation on railway property, engineering surveys or investment-return guarantees.

Development opportunities are qualitative study hypotheses. Land rights, demand, planning, environmental, operating and independent engineering assessments remain necessary. No unsourced ROI, cargo volume or travel-time estimate is supplied.

Sources and licensing

MapLibre GL JS, OpenFreeMap/OSM and the accepted project registries retain their existing credits. OSM-derived geometry is attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Official agency/operator references are linked for facts and context; linked material is not re-licensed as MR-S10 data.
